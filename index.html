📖 Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

  • Size: small–medium (about 20–30 lbs)
  • Personality: curious, merry, and great with families
  • Everyday care: daily walks, secure fence, regular ear checks

Golden Retriever

  • Size: large (55–75 lbs)
  • Personality: friendly, patient, and eager to please
  • Everyday care: lots of exercise, brushing 2–3 times a week

Poodle

  • Size: toy, miniature, or standard
  • Personality: smart, playful, and elegant
  • Everyday care: regular grooming, puzzle toys, daily walks
